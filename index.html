How to Play

  • Every gameweek a prediction form is posted in Discord. Predict the final score of every match on that week's coupon.
  • Pick a nickname and reuse the exact same one every week. It's how your points add up all season — and it keeps you anonymous on this table.
  • Deadline: predictions close at the first kickoff of the gameweek. Miss it and you sit that week out.
  • On the form you also nominate your Banker and choose whether to play your Joker (see rules below).

Scoring & House Rules

You predict a scoreline; the result is read from it: 1 home win · X draw · 3 away win.
  • +2 — correct outcome (your 1 / X / 3 matches the real result)
  • +4 — exact scoreline
  • +1 — close: you nailed one team's exact goals but not the full score — this counts even if the result went the other way (max +1 per game)
  • 0 — wrong result and neither team's goals right
  • 🏦 Banker — each week you nominate the one result you're most sure of. If you call that winner right, that match scores double (correct outcome banker = +4, exact-score banker = +8). Choose it wisely.
  • 🃏 Joker — you get 4 Jokers for the whole season. Play one on a gameweek to double your entire coupon that week — so save them for weeks you feel strong.
  • ⭐ Featured game — on selected big-match weeks the coupon adds two bonus calls on one marquee fixture: first team to score and first goalscorer, worth +5 each. They ride your joker too, but the banker doesn't apply to them.
Example: you predict Man Utd 1–3 Man City (an away win, “3”). Finishes 0–2 → still an away win → +2. Finishes exactly 1–3 → +4. Finishes 1–1 → wrong result, but you got Man Utd's 1 goal → +1. Finishes 2–0 → nothing right → 0.

Postponed matches score nothing until they're played, then get counted automatically whenever they happen. Most points at the end of the season wins the league. 🏆
